Hi, I am Sirui(Eric) Chen (陈思睿). A third year CS PhD student at Stanford advised by Professor [Karen Liu](https://tml.stanford.edu/people/karen-liu). I am generally interested in design robot control and planning algorithms for contact-rich tasks such as dexterous manipulation and humanoid wholebody loco-manipulation. I am also focusing on how to solve complex control problems in real robot combining different source human demonstrations as well as analytical modeling. I interned at Honda Research institute in summer 2024 advised by [Rana Soltani Zarrin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7NGxvsgAAAAJ&hl=en) and interned at Nvidia GEAR lab summer and fall 2025 advised by [Yuke Zhu](https://yukezhu.me/) and [Zhengyi Luo](https://www.zhengyiluo.com/). In my free time, I enjoy photography and hiking [here](https://500px.com/p/ericcsr?view=photos) are some photos I've taken. Feel free to reach out for internship oppotunity or discussing research ideas.
